# Evaluation sets — never used for training

Generated by `evals/generate.py` (fixed seed; answers computed by code): `math`, `finance`, `multilingual`,
`tools`, `general`, `style`.
`knowledge` (150 held-out MMLU questions across 57 subjects, MIT) comes from `evals/make_knowledge.py`.

**Lea's sets** (she writes them — they test what matters to her, so nobody else should):
- `lea_questions.jsonl` — questions you actually ask. One per line:
  `{"set": "lea", "id": "lea-1", "prompt": "…", "check": {"type": "contains", "value": "…"}}`
  (use `"check": {"type": "judge"}` when there's no single right answer — then it goes to your blind A/B).
- `conversations.jsonl` — person-to-person moments ("I'm stressed about exams", "guess what happened today"):
  `{"set": "conversations", "id": "conv-1", "prompt": "…", "check": {"type": "judge"}}` — rated by you in the blind A/B.
- `interests.jsonl` — messages where Sylvie *should* connect to one of your interests and ones where she should stay quiet:
  `{"set": "interests", "id": "int-1", "prompt": "…", "check": {"type": "aside", "value": true}}`.
- `memory.jsonl` — remembering what Lea said in earlier conversations (2026-11-12). `"earlier"` is a made-up
  earlier conversation ([her words, the answer], dated `"earlier_at"`), never Lea's own; `contains_any`
  accepts any of several wordings (an honest "I can't find it"), `not_contains` catches made-up details.

Run: `make eval` (current version) — results go to `~/Library/Application Support/Sylvie/evals/`.
